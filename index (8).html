How these numbers are calculated
  • Containers and orders: gross containers and distinct orders booked by the agent, counted on the booked date. Departments: IF Outbound (Clearwater) and Concentrix Philippines Outbound. Commercial outbound is excluded.
  • Opportunities assigned: Salesforce opportunities created in the period and owned by the agent today.
  • New: opportunities whose stage is New right now. For an earlier period this shows how many of those leads are still untouched today, not how many were untouched then.
  • Adjusted total: opportunities assigned minus New.
  • Touch rate: adjusted total divided by opportunities assigned. It is the share of assigned opportunities that have been worked at least once and are no longer in New. It depends on timing, because recent leads have had less time to be touched, so the Week view (opportunities from the previous Friday through Thursday) is the fairest comparison between agents and teams.
  • Close rate: orders divided by adjusted total. A single day is noisy because one day of orders is compared with one day of new leads; a week or longer is more reliable.
  • Conversion: containers divided by adjusted total.
  • 7-day cancel: orders cancelled within 7 days of the booked date, divided by orders booked at least 7 days before the latest booked date. Newer orders are left out so the rate is not understated, so Day and Week views near the end of the data often show no rate.
  • History before August 1: stored by month, not by day. A custom range that starts before August 1 is widened to whole months, and the range line above says so. Opportunities created before an agent's first month with an outbound sale are left out, because those leads were not worked by the outbound team.
  • Agents counted: only the 37 agents in the current data, so earlier periods do not include agents who have since left the team.
  • AOP: weekly targets from the AOP file, where "c" is containers and "o" is opportunities, and W54 is the week starting Monday, December 29, 2025. Each week is spread evenly over Monday to Friday, then added up for the selected period. "Percent to AOP" is actual divided by AOP through the selected end date. For month, quarter and year, the full-period figures compare against the whole period and show the daily pace needed to finish on target. The AOP covers the whole outbound department, so actuals include agents who have left, and the team filter does not change it.
  • Focus KPI: choose a KPI (or click a headline card) to switch the chart to that KPI by team, highlight its column, and sort agents by it. Counts are stacked bars; rates and containers per order are lines, worked out inside each bar's period. "Show or hide KPIs" removes KPIs from the cards and both tables.
  • Dials made: manual outbound calls placed by the agent, counted by call date from the call records. CSAT: share of customer survey responses marked satisfied. These surveys are sent after inbound calls, so an outbound agent only gets responses on the inbound calls they handle, which is only a few a month. CSAT cells with fewer than 5 surveys are grey italics with the survey count; the department and team totals are steadier.
  • Week view: the last completed Monday-to-Sunday week on or before the "Through" date. Orders, containers, dials and surveys are for those days; opportunities are those created the previous Friday through Thursday, because Friday is when the team works the funnel for the prior days' workload. The weekly tracker under "Progress to AOP" uses the same pairing. Day, Month, Quarter, Year to date and Custom views count opportunities by the day they were created. Opportunity counts are by current owner, so leads that supervisors or others have since taken over are not in an agent's count.
  • Team rosters: set in the ROSTER block at the top of the script. Agents under "Not on roster" are hidden unless you tick the box.
